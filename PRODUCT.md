# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitors are people looking to buy AEERNY mango juice. They need to understand the product and contact AEERNY to place an order.

## Product Purpose

AEERNY offers mango juice from Nyambaka, Cameroon. The website helps people learn about the juice and order it, while explaining the association and its work.

## Positioning

AEERNY's defining distinction is mango juice from Nyambaka. The user confirmed the current product, nutrition, recognition, and community-impact claims on the site as accurate.

## Operating Context

Orders and wholesale inquiries are directed to WhatsApp. The site also presents product information, the association's history, achievements, FAQs, and contact details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a static website made with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- Visitors can browse product and organization information and use WhatsApp links to contact AEERNY.
- Product information includes a nutrition table for a 250 ml serving.
- The site does not provide an on-site shopping cart or checkout.
- Keep existing product, nutrition, awards, media, and community-impact claims accurate when editing; the user confirmed the current claims are valid.

## Brand Commitments

- The organization and product brand is AEERNY.
- The website's audience-facing content is in French.
- The association is identified as the Association des Élèves et Étudiants Ressortissants de Nyambaka.

- Make it easy for juice buyers to understand the product and contact AEERNY to order.
