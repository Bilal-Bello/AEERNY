Our Story

Rooted in Nyambaka

AEERNY was born from a simple belief: the best mango juice comes from the best mangoes — and the best mangoes grow right here in Nyambaka.

We are the Association des Élèves et Étudiants Ressortissants de Nyambaka, a community-driven initiative turned juice brand. Every bottle we sell supports local farmers, local students, and local futures.

No factory shortcuts. No artificial additives. Just mangoes, care, and community pride.
